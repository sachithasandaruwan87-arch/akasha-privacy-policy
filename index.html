AKASHA MONEY FLOW — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

AKASHA MONEY FLOW is an offline-first personal money tracker published by AKASHA FORGE.

Data handled by the app

The app lets users enter balances, income, expenses, categories, budgets, goals, notes, currency and language preferences, profile settings and passcode settings.

Local data

The application is designed to store money-tracking data locally on the user's device. It does not require an AKASHA account and does not automatically upload transaction history to an AKASHA server.

Support and reports

The Support screen can open the user's email application. The user reviews and sends the email themselves. Messages are handled by the chosen email provider under its privacy terms. The app does not automatically attach or send financial records.

Analytics, advertising and sale of data

The release reviewed for this policy does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. AKASHA FORGE does not sell personal or financial data collected by the app.

Security and deletion

Money-tracking data is stored locally. Passcode-related protected settings use platform secure storage where implemented. Users are responsible for device security and backups. Removing data or uninstalling the app may remove local application data, subject to Android/device backup and restore behavior.

Children

The app is a general personal finance utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when functionality, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
